UNOMi Emotion Engine Showcase Media Folder
==========================================
Drop your sponsor-cleared videos (.mp4 or .webm) and screenshots (.png or .jpg)
directly into this folder (`assets/unomi/`) using these filenames:

1. assets/unomi/emotion-engine-demo.mp4   (Video bay 1 — Emotion Engine live demo)
2. assets/unomi/slate-panel.png           (Image bay 2 — SFacialExpressionPanel Slate UI)
3. assets/unomi/eye-jitter-demo.mp4       (Video bay 3 — Material UV eye jitter & lip sync)
4. assets/unomi/morph-blends.png          (Image bay 4 — Preset emotion blends & cross-DCC)

Tip: Keep videos short (10-30s, H.264 .mp4, under ~15 MB each) so they stream fast
on GitHub Pages and auto-play smoothly on the 3D gallery wall. You can also change
the filenames, types ('video' vs 'image'), titles, or captions in `src/content.js`
under `unomiMedia`.
